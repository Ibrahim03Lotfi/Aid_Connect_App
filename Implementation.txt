Athar (أثر) — Next-Gen Smart Humanitarian Operating System
Master Engineering Implementation Plan (Graduation Project)
System Overview & Architecture
Transforming the current basic prototype into an enterprise-grade digital operating system for a premier humanitarian relief organization ("منظمة أثر للإغاثة والتنمية").

The ecosystem comprises:

Laravel 12 Backend API & Web Command Center: Central database, business logic, queued AI jobs, and executive operations dashboard.
Flutter Mobile Application (Dual Mode):
Citizen / Donor Mode: Guided aid request wizard, case tracking, impact stories reels, and volunteer recruitment.
Volunteer Field Mode: NFC smart card scanner, offline-first aid distribution, field assessment visits, emergency missions, and certified volunteer passport.
Physical NFC Smart Aid Cards: Contactless cards for beneficiary families with printed QR fallback for instant, offline-capable verification.
Applied Multimodal AI Engine: Medical document auditing, shelter damage visual analysis, NLP urgency triage, fraud detection, and smart volunteer matchmaking.
Phase 1: Data Architecture & Backend Foundation (Laravel 12)
1.1 Database Schema Restructuring & Migrations
 Retain and enhance existing users, categories, governorates, and notifications.
 [NEW] beneficiaries / families Table:
id, head_of_family_name, national_id (unique), phone, family_members_count, governorate_id, address, latitude, longitude, vulnerability_score (1-100), nfc_card_uid (unique, nullable), qr_token (unique), status (active, suspended, pending_verification).

3.2 NFC Smart Card & Dual-Verification Engine
NFC Reader Integration (nfc_manager):
Background NFC listening when the "Aid Scanner" screen is active.
Tap physical NFC card to phone back ➔ Haptic vibration + Audio chime.
Instant modal with beneficiary details: Family name, members, allocation status.

Offline-First Synchronization:
Local caching of distribution records when internet drops in rural areas/camps.

Phase 4: Applied AI Intelligence Engine (Backend Services)
4.1 Printed Hospital Document & Invoice Auditor (Multimodal Vision AI)
Uses Gemini Vision API to analyze uploaded printed hospital invoices, MRI/CT scans, and pharmacy bills:
Extracts patient diagnosis, hospital name, invoice date, and total requested amount.
Mathematical integrity check: Validates that line items sum correctly to the total.
Auto-fills case metadata to eliminate manual admin data entry.
4.2 Shelter & Living Condition Visual Severity Analyzer
Volunteer takes photos of rooms (damp walls, cracked masonry, broken windows, lack of heating).
Vision model evaluates structural severity (Low, Moderate, Critical) and adds a visual assessment badge to the report.
4.3 Arabic NLP Urgency & Triage Classifier
Analyzes case titles, descriptions, and family situations.
Generates an Urgency Index (1 to 100):
Critical cases (infants needing emergency surgery, hypothermia risk) score 90+ and trigger priority alerts.
4.4 Perceptual Image Fingerprinting & Fraud Detection
Calculates image perceptual hash (pHash) on all uploaded photos.
Flags duplicate image submissions across different cases or past years to prevent recycled photo fraud.
4.5 Intelligent Volunteer Matchmaker
When an emergency mission is published (e.g. medical convoy or disaster relief), matches the required skills and closest GPS coordinates to recommend the top 5 volunteers.
